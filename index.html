Is it actually trading?

Daily volume as a share of market cap, log scale. Coins left of the amber line trade so little that their market cap says little about what you could sell for.

What the flags mean

  • Thin volume 24h volume under 1% of market cap.
  • Hot up 15% or more in 24h. Late buyers are often the exit liquidity.
  • Deep drawdown down 30% or more over 30 days.
  • Unlock overhang under 60% of total supply is circulating, so more can reach the market.

What the launch flags mean

  • Thin pool under $25K in the trading pool. A few trades move the price a long way and you may not get out. Small pool is under $150K.
  • Pool tiny vs value the pool is under 0.5% of the headline value (under 2% is a warning). Most of that value cannot be sold into.
  • No sells seen at least 30 buys and no sells in 24h. Some tokens can be bought but not sold.
  • Under 24h old Sell-heavy Volume far above pool Down 50%+ today are warnings, not verdicts.

        What counts as backing

        • Real access. A spot listing on a large regulated venue (Binance, Coinbase, Robinhood, SoFi, Upbit) puts the coin in front of many more buyers. It does not stop it falling.
        • More leverage. A futures listing adds traders who can be liquidated. Moves get bigger both ways.
        • Weak. Binance Alpha is not a listing. A Seed Tag is a risk label. A press release saying a Binance listing is coming is marketing.
        • Not backing. A name that mentions an exchange, or a caller's post. Wallets tied to callers have been alleged to trade first.
